Your position

How it works

Register the sailor before the trip. A smartphone sailor's GPS then tracks them at sea. A keypad sailor is tracked by satellite each time they use the voice line. Tap Start tracking when the canoe leaves. The phone saves its exact GPS position every 30 seconds, or sooner when it moves 25 m, and draws the trail on the map in blue.

Keep the screen on. A web app cannot read GPS once the screen locks, so leave the phone lit and dimmed in a waterproof pouch.

No signal at sea is fine. Points stay on the phone and are sent when the signal returns, once the control room address is set in the file.

SmartphoneLarge Go, Check in, Back and Help icons. Phone GPS draws a live trail on the map and sends when the signal returns.
Keypad phoneSMS with single-digit replies, no data needed. Every check-in saves a satellite tracker or cell tower position on the map.
Voice callBaak calls out and speaks the menu. The fisherman presses a number to go out, check in, come back or send HELP. Every key press saves a satellite position.
No phoneThe chief fisherman or beach committee logs departures and returns on a shared phone.

The phone on the left is a simulator of the voice line, played here as text. A real voice line needs a phone number from a voice provider that can place calls and play recorded prompts, in Fante, Twi, Ewe, Ga and English. Each time a keypad or voice-line sailor uses the voice line, the satellite tracker saves their position.
